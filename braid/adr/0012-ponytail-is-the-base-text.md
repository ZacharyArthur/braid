# Ponytail is the base text, not a source of ideas

braid's core, `review`, `audit` and `debt` are **based** on ponytail: its text at the pinned commit is imported verbatim in a commit of its own, and braid's edits (names and commands, the strands' blocks, its marker) follow in a second commit as renames and whole blocks between ponytail's sections. A sync is then a three-way merge (`git merge-file`) of ponytail's two pinned versions into braid's copy, not a rewrite. Derived rows, read for ideas only (ADR 0008's spirit), made every ponytail release a hand port, and ponytail 5 rewrote most of its text. Vendored rows (ADR 0003) allow only surgical edits, which the strands are not. braid started from ponytail; keeping its words keeps its tested behavior and each release's gains cheap to take. Supersedes the "derived" status of these four rows, and amends ADR 0007: braid's worst-case always-on cost stays within ~300 tokens of ponytail's worst case at the pin (its core, its codebase map at the cap, its auto-loading descriptions), checked by `scripts/check.cjs` against a figure re-measured on each sync. To fit, project-state names are clipped to 32 bytes, not 40 characters. Amended 2026-10-20: in `hooks/map.cjs`, whose output reaches every session start, braid may also change a single ponytail line for security or correctness (the first: `SAFE_PATH` widened from ASCII to letters, marks and digits in any script); the change that does so names the line, and a sync conflict on it is resolved by hand.
